A demo book with a 50,000 demo balance — nothing real is staked or won. Every race is a market and every horse has two sides: Yes buys the horse to win at its implied chance, No buys it to lose at one minus that — crowd prices from live odds with the bookmaker’s margin stripped out, so the game is exactly fair. A winning side returns stake ÷ price. Betting closes at post time and every bet is recorded to your account. Not betting, not advice.
