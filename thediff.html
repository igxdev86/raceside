The method, in the open: for every course where the EXP composite lost money over 12 months, ten simple alternative rules are replayed at SP \u2014 favourite, second favourite, top score, top RPR, top Topspeed, biggest parity gap, low draw among the front three, EXP-when-not-favourite, lowest number+draw sum, best trainer form in the market. Each candidate is chosen on the first 8 months only and then marked on the final 4 months it never saw. Only rules profitable in BOTH windows earn a VALIDATED badge and a place in the live picks. Courses where nothing survives say so \u2014 that honesty is the point. Even a validated rule is one split of one year: treat these as leads to keep marking forward, not certainties. \u00a31 level stakes throughout. Not advice.
