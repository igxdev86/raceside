Insights what the record says before today's nine

Favourites are landing. Friday went 4/5 to the market leader (Libertango 6/4, Emphatic 9/5, Sun Goddess 4/5, Hughes 4/5); the only miss was the Juvenile Turf, where the favourite Great Barrier Reef was scratched and the stable's second string Haffner won. Keeneland 2022 was 5/14 for favourites, so expect a couple of jollies to go down today — the record says it's the turf sprints and the fillies' turf where they fall.

Europe owns the Keeneland turf. 2022 here: six of seven turf races to Irish-breds, O'Brien 3 and Appleby 3, Moore rode all three Coolmore winners. Friday: O'Brien/Moore won both juvenile turf miles. Today's turf: Precise (O'Brien/Moore) F&M Turf, Diamond Necklace and Benvenuto Cellini (O'Brien) Turf, Notable Speech (Appleby/Buick) Mile, El Cordobes (Appleby) Turf, True Love and Puerto Rico (O'Brien) Mile.

Long home straight rewards closers on grass — Keeneland's turf stretch is one of the longest in North America. 2022 Juvenile and Juvenile Fillies were won from behind. Go against lone-speed types in the 1m–1½m turf races; the exception is the 5½f Turf Sprint, which favours early pace (Caravel bolted up at 42-1 in 2022).

Repeat bidders. Six of last year's winners are back: Forever Young (Classic, 2-1), Notable Speech (Mile, 7-5), Ethical Diamond (Turf, 10-1), Shisospicy (Turf Sprint, 15-1), Cy Fair (Turf Sprint, 8-1), Super Corredora (Distaff, 15-1). Splendora was scratched from the Distaff. Repeat Cup winners are rare but the Mile is the race it happens most (Goldikova ×3, Wise Dan ×2, Modern Games/Notable Speech lineage for Appleby) — Notable Speech is the live one.

Japan on dirt. Forever Young won last year's Classic (Yahagi/Sakai) and is favourite to repeat; T O Elvis (7-2, Sprint) and Lord Fons (Dirt Mile) carry the same angle. Racing Post noted no European runners on dirt again, so dirt is USA v Japan.

Prices run big on this card. 2025 Del Mar average winning odds were ~7/1 with 20-1 and 27-1 winners in the Juvenile Fillies Turf and Turf; 2022 Keeneland threw a 42-1 Turf Sprint winner. Each-way or place-only on 8-1+ turf runners has paid at both venues.

Short ones to trust, if the pattern holds: Englishman 4/5 (Sprint, 4-for-4 this year), Nitrogen 6/5 v Fully Subscribed 8/5 (Distaff — two-horse race), Further Ado 2-1 (Dirt Mile, Cox). Friday's dirt favourites both won by 1½–2¼ lengths.
