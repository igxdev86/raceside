How this ledger works · read once
Read this first: Read this first: this ledger replays the backtest rule — SP prices and the lean score (RPR, Topspeed, trainer form) that history actually stores. THE EXP's live page uses card prices and the full score with form, freshness and draw. So a day here will NOT match the live page's tiles for the same day — different picks in some races, different prices on all of them, sometimes a different race count. Both are honest; they're answering different questions: the live page marks the full rule forward, this ledger measures the skeleton backward. The most recent day or two can also lag a few hours behind.

Each day: every EXP pick of the day (the same picks the 12-month backtest makes — market × icon boosts × parity, settled at SP), full-cover multiples of the chosen size at £1 a line (switch fold with the chips above). UUU tracks the View's ghosts: in each race with a U — a non-icon the market prices like an icon (top-3 of the betting at 8/1 or shorter, inside the icons' odds range, scoring well below the frame or unrated) — the shortest-priced such horse is the selection. SIPA is the PAINT-top selection kept only when its sire strikes 15%+ over 50+ runs — the trend leader with a proven bloodline behind it. SIRE+15 keeps only EXP picks whose sire strikes at 15%+ over 50+ runs (sharing the TOP SIRES build — give it a minute on first use, or open the sires page once). The PAINT TOP tab switches the selection itself: the horse the PAINT trend ranks #1 in each race (replayed with slot, icon-band and draw tables built from the data window), not the EXP pick. BETS is C(picks,k) — the day's stake in pounds. Landed bets are every k-way combination drawn entirely from the day's winners; RETURN is their combined payout. The stake varies wildly with the size of the card — a 35-pick Saturday costs £6,545, a quiet Monday £120 — so read the ROI, not just the totals, and remember that fold P/L is the singles edge raised to the fold's power: a few loaded days will dominate everything. Best and worst days tinted. Not advice.
