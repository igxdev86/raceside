Each day: every EXP pick of the day (the same picks the 12-month backtest makes — market × icon boosts × parity, settled at SP), full-cover trebles at £1 a line. BETS is C(picks,3) — the day's stake in pounds. Landed trebles are every 3-way combination drawn entirely from the day's winners; RETURN is their combined payout. The stake varies wildly with the size of the card — a 35-pick Saturday costs £6,545, a quiet Monday £120 — so read the ROI, not just the totals, and remember that treble P/L is the singles edge cubed: a few loaded days will dominate everything. Best and worst days tinted. Not advice.
