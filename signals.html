THE SIGNALSrules that held out of sample

A search over combinations of the site’s signals — best-in at the weights, pairs, people strikes, OR, minus rank, price, race type — for rules that beat the starting price. Rules are found on January–June and then judged only on races they never saw, from July onwards. People stats are built day by day so no race sees its own future. What survives is listed with today’s picks. The list will change month to month; a rule that keeps surviving is a signal, one that appears once is weather. Not advice.
