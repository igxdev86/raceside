Every GB/IRE runner grouped by sire, settled at SP with £1 level stakes. A/E is actual wins divided by market-expected wins (the sum of 1/SP across the progeny's runs). The overround lives inside that expectation, so the break-even A/E sits below 1.0 — judge each sire against the ALL-SIRES baseline shown above the table, not against 1.0. An A/E meaningfully above baseline on hundreds of runs means the market persistently underrates the stock; the ROI column says whether that was backable. Tap a sire for flat/jumps and distance-band splits. Small samples lie — the min-runs chips exist for a reason. Not advice.
