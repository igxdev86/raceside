Every GB/IRE ride and run over 12 months, settled at SP with £1 level stakes. A/E is actual wins divided by market-expected wins (the sum of 1/SP across the progeny's runs). The overround lives inside that expectation, so the break-even A/E sits below 1.0 — judge each sire against the ALL-SIRES baseline shown above the table, not against 1.0. An A/E meaningfully above baseline on hundreds of rides means the market persistently underrates that person's mounts; the ROI column says whether that was backable.
