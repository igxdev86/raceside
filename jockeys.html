● gold dot = steady: 200+ rides, busy in 9+ months, a winner in every busy month — never goes missing.
Next ride: blue today · grey tomorrow · — not booked. Travel: grey short hop · amber 2–3½ h from yesterday’s track · red 3½ h+ haul (estimate).
A/E: green above baseline — mounts win more than their prices predicted (market underrates them) · red below — market over-bets them. This is the money column; strike measures fame.
Odds profile (tap a name): same green/red per price band · ★ = their best band, where the market misprices them most.

Every GB/IRE ride and run over 12 months, settled at SP with £1 level stakes. A/E is actual wins divided by market-expected wins (the sum of 1/SP across the progeny's runs). The overround lives inside that expectation, so the break-even A/E sits below 1.0 — judge each sire against the ALL-SIRES baseline shown above the table, not against 1.0. An A/E meaningfully above baseline on hundreds of rides means the market persistently underrates that person's mounts; the ROI column says whether that was backable. Tap a row for the flat/jumps split. Small samples lie — the min-runs chips exist for a reason. Not advice.
