Questions

Is this a tipping service?

No. The terminal shows you a model, the live market and a verified record, and lets you make your own call. Nothing is sold as a "sure thing".

What does "verified at the off" actually mean?

When a race goes off, the terminal locks the prices and scores it was showing and stamps the time, to the second. Results are judged against that locked snapshot — never against hindsight.

Whose prices are shown?

Ladbrokes where they've priced the race, with a market fallback before they have. One firm, consistently, so the record means something.

What racing is covered?

Every GB and Irish meeting, flat and jumps, every day.
