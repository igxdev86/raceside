Every top-OR most-minus pick of the year in time order. Each row answers: if you waited for exactly k straight losses and then bet £1 on the next pick, what happened? Strike, what the SPs implied, A/E, and the £1 ROI at that entry. If picks are independent, every row reads about the same and there is no optimum pounce — that flat line would itself be the finding. With this many rows, the best one always looks good partly by luck; trust the whole shape, not the single shiniest row. Not advice.
