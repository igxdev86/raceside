building the wall…

Every GB & IRE race today as a micro racecard: the three icon picks (🏆 top base score · ♦️ second · 🔹 third) with current best price and score, plus the market-implied chance an icon wins (the icons' share of the overround-normalised book), F marking the market favourite and U marking ghosts — non-icon horses in the market's top three at 8/1 or shorter, priced inside the icons' odds range, while scoring clearly below the frame or carrying no ratings: the market treats them as contenders, the score doesn't, and one of the two knows something. Settled tiles show the winner with its rank badge, SP, and Z when the top-scored horse won. The italic figure after each score is parity — the score that horse's market rank implies (the field's own scores dealt out in betting order). Green = the ratings rate it 4+ points above its price (value shape); red = the market rates it 4+ points above its score (ghost shape). On the Yesterday tab, tiles are rebuilt from results: prices are SPs and scores lean on RPR + Topspeed alone (form and trainer data aren't in the results feed), so treat yesterday's scores as the leaner engine ruler. Scores otherwise are the 62-pt static build (RPR + TS + C&D + trainer 14-day + form + freshness, field-relative). On Today the wall re-pulls prices every 2½ minutes while open: ▲ green = backed in, ▼ red = drifted (8%+ move since the last cycle; hover for the exact prices). On flat races the final column is draw bias: how often the winner came from that horse's draw third (LOW/MID/HIGH stalls) over 12 months of races at the same course, distance band and field size \u2014 33% means no bias; green 38%+, red 28% or under; blank when under 20 comparable races. Not advice.
