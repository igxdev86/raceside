Movement is measured against the first price this device saw today (your baseline — open the page early for a longer view). Prices are best available across the card's bookmakers, so some movement is one firm pushing out rather than the whole market. Steamers shortened the most in percentage terms; drifters lengthened. Refreshes every 2 minutes; settled races grey out and show the winner. A steamer is the market telling you someone's confident — it is not, on its own, a reason to follow: backed-in horses win more often but at prices that have already absorbed the confidence.
