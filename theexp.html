One pick per race: the EXP composite — market probability boosted by icon status (top score ×1.15, other icons ×1.08), 12-month draw bias (clamped), and value-shaped parity (×1.06); fancied non-icons carry no penalty (the ghost lesson). Multiples are full cover: every combination of that size across all settled picks at £1 a line, settled at card prices (SP on Yesterday) — the bet counts show what full cover actually costs. The TOP-5 ACCA is the five strongest picks by composite as a single £1 accumulator. BY COURSE shows the settled record and £1-singles P/L per meeting, best to worst. The 12-month table replays the EXP core (market × icon boosts × parity) at SP with the same leak-free score the engines use — no draw factor (distance isn't warehoused) and no form/freshness inputs, so it's the composite's skeleton, not its full live rule. Courses need 10+ picks to appear; sorted by ROI — small samples near the bottom of that range are noise, so weight the big-number rows. A green-tinted STEADY row means the profit is boring in the best way: 10+ meetings, at least half of them green, and still profitable with its single best day removed — the shape a real edge takes, rather than one spike carrying a year. “vs fav” is EXP's ROI minus just-backing-the-favourite's ROI at that course — the honest measure of whether EXP adds anything there. Not advice; a system being marked in public.
